# MiniMax / Hailuo pack — এগারোটা দশ (পার্ট ৩/৩ · ফাইনাল)

Vertical 1080×1920, Hailuo 2.3 image-to-video, one voice. Same two-layer look as parts 1–2, plus
one new colour for this part: **fire**. It is the only warm thing in the film that is not a lamp.

**Global style token (scenes):**

> flat vector noir illustration, thick dark outlines, solid flat colours only, no gradients,
> simple geometric shapes, minimal shading; monsoon palette of ink navy, ash grey, bone white, one
> amber lamp, and one flat fire orange in the flashbacks; rain as flat white dashes. No text, no
> watermark.

**Brief rule for this part:** the 2005 fire is shown as **smoke, windows and silhouettes behind a
grille** — no bodies, no flames on people, no gore. The old man is never shown in the same frame as
the fire.

**Negative prompt:**

> photorealistic, 3d render, cgi, gradient shading, airbrush, lens flare, halftone dots, blurry,
> soft focus, text, subtitles, watermark, logo, extra limbs, extra fingers, deformed hands,
> morphing faces, flicker, jump cut, camera shake, gore, blood, burning bodies, screaming faces,
> neon colours, daylight.
