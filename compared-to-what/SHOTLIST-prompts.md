# কিসের তুলনায়? — shot list for a video generator

24 shots, 2:01 total, 16:9. Built with your eight-question formula: one action per clip,
one style line and one character block pasted into every prompt, camera language only,
no text and no speech asked of the generator.

Everything the generator must not invent — the prices, the title, the moral, the narration —
is listed at the end as post work, because those are exactly the things it garbles.

---

## The two locked blocks

**STYLE LINE** — copy this, unchanged, into every prompt:

> Stylized 3D storybook animation, muted autumn palette, soft golden-hour light,
> matte low-poly volumes, shallow depth of field, slow camera moves, gentle film grain.

**CHARACTER BLOCK** — copy this, unchanged, into every prompt:

> Bruno: young brown rabbit, cream belly, small green backpack, red coin purse, long upright ears.
> Olive: old barn owl, round wire glasses, worn grey scarf, speckled cream-and-brown feathers, one missing tail feather.
> Hedgehog: old hedgehog shopkeeper, dull brown quills, canvas apron, round snout, no spectacles.

**WORLD BLOCK** — copy into any prompt that shows the town:

> Small animal town, cobbled square, warm plaster shopfronts with pitched roofs, bare autumn
> trees with orange canopies, one black lamp post.

## Keeping it consistent

1. Generate **stills first** — one clean turn of Bruno on a neutral square, one of Olive,
   one of the hedgehog. Lock them. Every clip is image-to-video from those stills.
2. Where the tool supports it, feed the **last frame** of shot N as the first frame of N+1
   when the pair share a set-up (03→04, 06→07, 11→12, 20→21).
3. Same seed family for the whole film if your tool exposes seeds.
4. Keep shot lengths at 4–6 s. Anything past 8 s starts to drift in the face and the props.
5. If a shot comes back wrong, change **one** field. Changing the action and the camera
   together tells you nothing about which one broke it.
6. Reject any clip with readable lettering. Even a perfect shot with garbled text costs more
   to fix than the shot is worth — put the price card on it in the edit instead.
