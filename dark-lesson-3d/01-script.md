# অন্ধকার পাঠ · DARK LESSON #01 — "ধার করা আলো"
### A 3D-anime style dark lesson in Bangla · 1080×1920 · 24 fps · 59 s

**Logline:** A boy finds a black lantern in the alley. It gives him everything he asks for —
and takes one memory per wish. By the end he owns the world and doesn't remember his mother's face.

**The lesson (one line):** যে আলো ধার করা, সে পথ দেখায় — কিন্তু তোমাকে চিনতে পারে না।
*Borrowed light shows you the road. It never learns who you are.*

Nothing here is a borrowed design. The three of them are built from spheres, cones and boxes
in `cast.py`, so every gesture is a number, not a sprite.

Palette: ink `#0E0E11` · brick `#131317` · cold light `#8FAEFF` · **the one warm accent
`#FFB060`** — only Ayan's own candle is ever allowed to be warm.

## The CTA — four beats, cheapest ask last

```
অন্ধকার পাঠ                     ← the brand line: this is episode 01 of a series
কোন স্মৃতি তুমি কখনো বেচবে না?    ← a question the viewer can answer about themselves
যে আলো ধার করা, সে তোমাকে চেনে না।  ← the reason the answer matters
কমেন্ট করো: একটা স্মৃতি           ← the cheapest action, and it seeds the comment section
[ ফলো করো ]                     ← one filled shape: the only button on the card
প্রতি শুক্রবার · নতুন পাঠ          ← turns a follow into a habit
```

Why this one works where "subscribe please" doesn't:

1. **It asks for a memory, not a click.** The comment prompt is personal, so people answer
   with stories — and a comment section full of stories is the strongest signal the
   algorithm has.
2. **The comment is on-topic.** Every "একটা স্মৃতি" comment is a mini-testimonial for the series;
   the next episode's comment section inherits it.
3. **One button.** The pill is the only filled shape; the eye lands on it at phone brightness.
4. **A cadence.** "প্রতি শুক্রবার" converts one watch into a returning habit; without it a follow
   is a coin flip.
5. **Spoken as well as written.** The last VO says the same three things, because about half of
   vertical viewers never read the card.

> Do **not** put an engagement-exchange link (Like4Like & co.) on this card. It breaks platform
> policy, it teaches the algorithm that your viewers don't finish videos, and it asks for a click
> your audience cannot give.

