# MiniMax / Hailuo shot pack — স্পেস স্টোরি · অতিথি ৩

Fourteen clips for Hailuo 2.3 (image-to-video, vertical). Cut on the in/out times, lay
`build/mix.wav` under it (or the ten `audio/sceneNN.mp3` at the `plan.py` starts), and the
narration lands exactly where it lands in the local render.

**Global style token** (end of every still prompt):

> 1970s science-fiction comic book illustration, thick confident black ink outlines of even
> weight, flat poster colours only — mustard yellow, burnt orange, cream, teal, steel grey —
> solid black shadows, one flat highlight band per surface, a hint of off-register print at the
> edges, faint paper tooth. No gradients, no airbrush, no photorealism, no halftone dots.

**Global negative prompt:**

> photorealistic, 3d render, cgi, glossy skin, blurry, soft focus, gradient shading, airbrush,
> halftone dots, lens flare, modern digital look, text, subtitles, watermark, logo, extra limbs,
> extra fingers, deformed hands, morphing faces, flicker, jump cut, camera shake, crowd, earth
> in the sky, two moons, faces of the crew changing between shots.

**Camera rules:** 1080×1920, one slow move per clip — push-in ≤ 4 % or 2° drift. People act with
neck, shoulders and one hand; nobody walks except where the table says so.

**Continuity:** same ship, same five crew, same two guests as parts 1–2. The guest is grey-teal,
three long fingers, two big black almond eyes, fine spines down the spine; the small one is the
same design and is now about chest-high on a human. The hold has rope-lashed crates, a clipboard
on a nail and one swinging caged lamp; the special crate is plain wood, nail-shut, warm along its
seams. The jar is glass, three-quarters full of damp red soil, masking tape on the side. Nobody
wears a helmet inside; Mars is a small red dot that never moves.

## 01 · window (5.5 s)

**Still:** inside a small deep-space ship, a big oval observation window with a riveted steel
frame, a low padded bench, a handrail, a wall lamp off; through the glass black space, stars, a
faint violet nebula and a small red planet far behind. No people. + global style token

**Motion:** starlight only; the planet does not move. Camera pushes in 3 %.

## 02 · the hold (5.0 s)

**Still:** a cramped cargo hold, crates lashed to the deck with rope, a cargo net, a hand winch, a
clipboard on a nail, pipes across the ceiling, one caged trouble lamp swinging, and in the back
corner a plain wooden crate turned away from the others, sealed, a faint warm glow along its
seams. No people. + global style token

**Motion:** the lamp swings a few degrees, its shadows crossing the crates; rope creaks. Camera
drifts forward 2 %.

## 03 · s1 cabin, five taps and one (9.4 s)

**Still:** the small cabin at night; the doctor in a cream smock over a teal coverall standing at
the left with her medi-kit; at the right the grey-teal guest, tall, and beside it the small one,
now chest-high, one three-fingered hand raised toward the round porthole. + global style token

**Motion:** the small one taps the glass five times, slowly, then once more; the tall one watches
without moving; the doctor turns her head. Camera pushes in 3 %.

## 04 · s2 the sixth tap (8.3 s)

**Still:** the oval window; the commander in her cream flight vest at the left, the tall guest
centre with one hand on the glass, the small one beside it; stars beyond.
+ global style token

**Motion:** one single hard tap — the sixth — lands with no one having moved to make it; everyone
freezes; a hairline of frost spreads from the point of contact. Camera holds still, then drifts
right 2°.

## 05 · s3 thermal scan: eight (6.9 s)

**Still:** the bridge close on the console: a small thermal screen showing eight warm blobs in a
row, four chunky keys, the comms tech leaning over it with his notebook, the pilot behind him.
+ global style token

**Motion:** the tech presses a key, the screen refreshes and still shows eight; his pencil stops.
Camera pushes in 3 %.

## 06 · s4 torch in the hold (10.3 s)

**Still:** the cargo hold, a hard torch beam cutting through dust, the doctor walking slowly
between the lashed crates, her long shadow swinging across the walls, the far corner still dark.
+ global style token

**Motion:** she takes three slow steps toward the back, the beam sweeping the crates and settling
on a plain wooden one; dust turns in the light. Camera tracks her 2 %.

## 07 · s5 the guest blocks the way (6.8 s)

**Still:** the hold; the grey-teal guest standing squarely in front of the commander and the
doctor, arms slightly out, its body between them and the wooden crate behind it; the two women
lit by torchlight. + global style token

**Motion:** the guest leans a few degrees toward the crate as the women step forward, and does not
yield; its head turns to each of them in turn. Camera pushes in 3 %.

## 08 · s6 the lid comes off (10.5 s)

**Still:** close on the opened wooden crate: lid levered off and leaning, straw packing, and
nested in the straw a big glass jar three-quarters full of damp red Martian soil, masking tape
on the glass; the commander crouched at the left, the guest standing at the right; a warm glow
comes up out of the soil. + global style token

**Motion:** the last nail gives, the lid lands, dust jumps; the glow in the soil pulses once, like
breathing. Camera holds still, pushes in 2 % at the end.

## 09 · s7 the label (7.1 s)

**Still:** very close on the jar in the straw, the strip of masking tape turned to camera with
faint handwriting on it, the doctor's face above and behind it, half lit, looking down. + global
style token

**Motion:** her hand comes into frame and stops short of the glass; the light in the soil rises
and falls. Camera drifts down 2°.

## 10 · s8 the confession (8.5 s)

**Still:** the cabin; the doctor standing with her head down, hands at her sides, the commander
facing her, the bare bulb between them. + global style token

**Motion:** the doctor raises her head, says one line, and looks away; the commander does not
answer, only breathes. Camera pushes in 4 %.

## 11 · s9 three of them (10.8 s)

**Still:** the hold, quiet now: the tall guest and the small one standing close together beside
the opened crate, the commander standing back with the torch lowered, warm glow on the deck.
+ global style token

**Motion:** the small one leans into the tall one's side; the tall one's three-fingered hand rests
on the crate; the torch beam lowers slowly. Camera drifts left 2°.

## 12 · s10 six taps at the glass (16.3 s)

**Still:** the oval window at last light: the tall guest and the small one at the glass, the
commander and the comms tech a little behind them, the jar held carefully in the commander's
arms; stars and the small red planet beyond. + global style token

**Motion:** at 6.0 s the tall guest taps five times, evenly; at 8.9 s once more; at 11.1 s the
small one taps, so softly it is almost nothing. Nobody speaks. Camera pushes in 3 %.

## 13 · out — empty window (2.5 s)

**Still:** the same window, the deck empty, the bench, the handrail, stars. + global style token

**Motion:** starlight only; the frame brightens half a stop.

## 14 · card (2.8 s)

**Still:** flat cream card with a hairline ink frame; heavy Bengali title **অতিথি ৩** over a
burnt-orange rule, then **স্পেস স্টোরি · অতিথি ৩**, then two small lines:
**ওরা একটা পরিবার পাহারা দিচ্ছিল।** / **তিন সপ্তাহ, নিঃশব্দে।** Paper tooth only.

**Motion:** none. Fade up 0.6 s, hold, fade out 1.0 s.
