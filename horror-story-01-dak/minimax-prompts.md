# MiniMax / Hailuo shot pack — ভয়ের গল্প · পর্ব ১ «ডাক»

Fourteen clips for Hailuo 2.3 (image-to-video, vertical). Cut on the in/out times, lay
`build/mix.wav` under them (or the ten `audio/sceneNN.mp3` at the `plan.py` starts), and the
narration sits where it sits in the local render.

**Global style token** (end of every still prompt):

> 1970s Bengali horror comic book illustration, thick confident black ink outlines of even
> weight, flat poster colours only — amber, burnt umber, mustard, deep olive green, bone cream —
> solid black shadows, one flat highlight band per surface, a hint of off-register print at the
> edges, faint paper tooth. No gradients, no airbrush, no photorealism, no halftone dots.

**Global negative prompt:**

> photorealistic, 3d render, cgi, glossy skin, blurry, soft focus, gradient shading, airbrush,
> halftone dots, lens flare, modern digital look, text, subtitles, watermark, logo, extra limbs,
> extra fingers, deformed hands, morphing faces, flicker, jump cut, camera shake, gore, blood,
> visible monster, ghost face, zombies, daylight, neon colours.

**Camera rules:** 1080×1920, one slow move per clip — push-in ≤ 4 % or 2° drift. Nothing
handheld, no whip-pans, no jump scares cut into the middle of a clip. The horror is in stillness:
the door, the clock, the gap under it.

**Continuity:** one village house — mud walls, tin roof, one veranda post pair, one window with a
hurricane lamp behind it, one heavy planked door with an iron sliding bolt and a handprint in the
dust, one khat bed, one stopped wall clock with a moth behind the glass. Rafi is the same young
man in every clip: cream shirt, dark lungi, gamchha on the shoulder, bare feet, three days of
stubble. Mother is never seen — only heard. Nothing supernatural is ever fully shown.

## 01 · the yard, the footprints (5.6 s)

**Still:** a village yard at two in the morning seen from the veranda steps: wet earth, a bamboo
fence, a broken clay pot, tall grass bending in wind, an old mango tree, and a single pair of
bare footprints in the wet dust walking toward the door and stopping at the threshold. No people.
+ global style token

**Motion:** grass bends; the moon goes behind thin cloud and comes back; the dust settles. Camera
pushes in 3 % toward the footprints.

## 02 · the house, one lit window (5.2 s)

**Still:** the house from the yard: mud walls, corrugated tin roof, a narrow veranda with two
posts, one small window glowing warm amber, the door shut and bolted, a clay pot and a broom by
the step, a neem tree leaning over the roof. No people. + global style token

**Motion:** the lamp behind the window flickers twice; the tree moves; a moth crosses the light.
Camera drifts right 2°.

## 03 · s1 the door, and the feet under it (8.4 s)

**Still:** from inside the room, the heavy planked door filling the frame — iron studs, a big
sliding bolt, handprints in the dust on the wood, a dark hairline gap under it, and in that gap
the shadow of two bare feet standing outside, not moving. + global style token

**Motion:** the feet do not move at all; the thin line of lamp light under the door trembles as
if someone on the other side shifted their weight. Camera pushes in 3 %.

## 04 · s2 Rafi at the house (10.2 s)

**Still:** Rafi, 24, cream shirt and dark lungi, gamchha on the shoulder, barefoot, standing in
the yard before the lit veranda with one hand at his chest, frightened and tired.
+ global style token

**Motion:** the veranda lamp flickers; he turns his head slowly to the closed door and back to
camera; wind moves the grass around his ankles. Camera pushes in 4 %.

## 05 · s3 the room, the lamp (9.8 s)

**Still:** the single room: whitewashed stained walls, a wooden khat bed with a folded quilt, a
hurricane lamp on a small table throwing amber light, a heavy door at the back, a stopped wall
clock, a tin trunk, a framed photograph turned to the wall. + global style token

**Motion:** the lamp flame dips and the room's shadows swing; a cobweb moves; nothing else.
Camera drifts left 2°.

## 06 · s4 close on Rafi (7.0 s)

**Still:** Rafi standing in the lamp light, three-quarters towards camera, one hand at his
throat, eyes wet, listening to the room. + global style token

**Motion:** he breathes twice, swallows, does not blink; his shadow on the wall breathes with
him. Camera pushes in 4 %.

## 07 · s5 two o'clock (6.1 s)

**Still:** an old wall clock seen straight on, cracked glass, brass hands stopped exactly at two,
a moth pinned behind the glass, dust on the rim, a cobweb in one corner, peeling whitewash
around it. + global style token

**Motion:** nothing moves in the clock — but the clock's shadow on the wall shifts a millimetre,
as if something passed behind the lamp. Camera holds absolutely still.

## 08 · s6 the voice outside (9.7 s)

**Still:** tight on the inside of the door: the iron bolt, the handprints, the gap under it with
the two shadows of feet still there, a little closer to the threshold than before.
+ global style token

**Motion:** dust trickles from the bottom of the door; the bolt trembles once in its keeper as
weight leans on the other side. Camera pushes in 2 %.

## 09 · s7 he walks to the door (10.1 s)

**Still:** the room from a little further back; Rafi half-turned away from camera, walking
towards the door, the lamp behind him throwing his long shadow up the wall ahead of him.
+ global style token

**Motion:** four slow barefoot steps across the mud floor; his shadow reaches the door before he
does; he stops a hand's width from it. Camera tracks 2°.

## 10 · s8 under the bed (11.5 s)

**Still:** low angle on the floor beside the khat bed: the black gap underneath, a rolled quilt,
a chamber pot, a child's rubber ball, dust curling in a thin breath of cold air — and nothing
visible in the dark. + global style token

**Motion:** the dust curl moves as if something exhaled; the quilt edge lifts a centimetre and
settles. Camera creeps forward 4 %, slowly, into the gap.

## 11 · s9 it stops pretending (7.9 s)

**Still:** the same door, closer and harder this time: the handprints seem wet now, the gap under
it dark and empty — the feet are gone. + global style token

**Motion:** one heavy impact from the other side; the bolt jumps in its keeper; the lamp behind
camera gutters. Camera holds still through the impact, then pushes in 2 %.

## 12 · s10 the door was never shut (9.9 s)

**Still:** from inside: the heavy door standing ajar on its own, the iron bolt hanging loose, a
wedge of cold blue night and tall grass visible through the opening, a rectangle of moonlight
across the floor, leaves blown in. + global style token

**Motion:** the door swings open another few inches, unhurried; wind moves the leaves inside; the
lamp behind camera dies at the end of the clip. Camera pushes in 3 %.

## 13 · out — the empty yard (2.6 s)

**Still:** the yard, empty; the footprints now lead away from the door toward the fence, and the
house window is dark. + global style token

**Motion:** grass moves; nothing else. The frame darkens half a stop.

## 14 · card (3.4 s)

**Still:** flat cream card with a hairline ink frame; heavy Bengali title **ডাক** over a
burnt-orange rule, then **ভয়ের গল্প · পর্ব ১**, then two small lines:
**দরজাটা কি সত্যিই বন্ধ ছিল?** / **একবার ভেবে দেখুন।** Paper tooth only.

**Motion:** none. Fade up 0.6 s, hold, fade out 1.2 s — the card should be the only silence in
the film that is safe.

## Voice

One narrator, all ten lines, Bengali, quiet and close, as if speaking into the listener's ear.
Line 8 (the whisper from under the bed) is narrated, never acted — the narrator reports it; the
listener supplies the voice. Do not cut a line at a shot change.
