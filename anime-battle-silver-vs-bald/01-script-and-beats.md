# "SILVER vs BALD" — 2-minute anime battle short

**Format:** 1920×1080 (16:9), 30fps, 2:00, English dub-style dialogue, synthesized score + SFX
**Style:** modern shonen action anime — cel-shaded 2D, speed lines, impact frames, dust and debris
**Note on characters:** original designs built on the *Goku* and *Saitama* archetypes (invincible spiky-haired martial artist vs bored one-punch hero). Not the licensed characters.

## Character lock-sheet (must stay identical in every frame)

**SILVER (A)** — tall, lean-muscular martial artist; spiky silver-white hair standing straight up; glowing cyan eyes; deep crimson sleeveless gi over a black undershirt; dark blue sash; wrapped forearms. Power aura: electric cyan.

**BALD (B)** — average build, completely bald, blank half-lidded bored expression; plain light-grey jumpsuit; white cape; yellow gloves; stands with hands in pockets. Serious only once, at the very end.
