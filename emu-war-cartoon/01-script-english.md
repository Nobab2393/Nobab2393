## Facts (verified)
- Nov 1932, Western Australia (Campion district). ~20,000 emus migrating into wheat farms.
- Military deployed two Lewis guns + ~10,000 rounds, led by Major G.P.W. Meredith.
- First operation: ~6 days, roughly 50–1,000 birds killed (estimates vary wildly); army withdrew.
- Second operation: ~2 weeks, only a few hundred confirmed; emus dispersed. Widely remembered as "the war the emus won."
