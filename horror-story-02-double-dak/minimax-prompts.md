# MiniMax / Hailuo shot pack — ভয়ের গল্প · পর্ব ২ «দুইবার ডাক»

Fifteen clips for Hailuo 2.3 (image-to-video, vertical 1080×1920). Cut on the in/out times, then
lay `build/mix.wav` (or the ten tempo-set clips in `build/sped/` at the `plan.py` starts) under
them. Shot 10b/10c is a screen UI, not a generated shot — composite it or shoot it as a real
phone screen; the name change must land on the beat.

**Global style token** (end of every still prompt):

> 1970s Bengali horror comic book illustration, thick confident black ink outlines of even
> weight, flat poster colours only — amber, burnt umber, mustard, deep olive green, bone cream,
> with cold blue night and cold blue phone light — solid black shadows, one flat highlight band
> per surface, a hint of off-register print, faint paper tooth. No gradients, no airbrush, no
> photorealism, no halftone dots.

**Global negative prompt:**

> photorealistic, 3d render, cgi, glossy skin, blurry, soft focus, gradient shading, airbrush,
> halftone dots, lens flare, modern digital look, text, subtitles, watermark, extra limbs, extra
> fingers, deformed hands, morphing faces, flicker, jump cut, camera shake, blood, gore, wounds,
> visible face on the creature, daylight, neon colours.

**Camera rules:** one slow move per clip — push-in ≤ 4 %. Horror lives in stillness: the thing
never rushes, the door never slams, the camera never shakes. Only the last two clips are edited
fast.

**Continuity:** the same village room and door as part 1 (mud walls, tin roof, khat bed,
hurricane lamp, heavy planked door with iron bolt). Rafi is the same young man: cream shirt, dark
lungi, gamchha, bare feet, stubble. The creature is never seen whole and never shows a face —
long wet black hair over it, pale waterlogged skin, black swollen feet, no wounds. The city flat
is small, narrow, one barred window, one bed, a phone on the floor. Phone screen: black
background, silhouette avatar, name in white — **মা**, later **রাফি**.

## 01 · p0 the room as it was (3.3 s)

**Still:** the single-room village house: whitewashed stained walls, khat bed, a hurricane lamp
burning warm on a small table, a stopped wall clock, the heavy door standing ajar, a hard wedge
of amber floor light against cold blue outside. Empty. + global style token

**Motion:** the lamp flame dips twice and the shadows swing; a cobweb moves. Push in 2 %.

## 02 · p1 the handprints (2.7 s)

**Still:** the same room at first light, grey dawn through the doorway, lamp dead and cold, and
on the dusty floor two clear handprints pressed into the dust with drag marks leading away from
the bed. + global style token

**Motion:** dust drifts across the prints; nothing else. Camera holds still, then creeps 2 % in.

## 03 · s1 the open door, nobody (7.6 s)

**Still:** from inside: the heavy door wide open onto tall grass, a neem tree, a thin moon, a
loose iron bolt; a pale rectangle of moonlight and a few blown-in leaves across the threshold.
Nobody there. + global style token

**Motion:** grass bends, a gust lifts two leaves over the sill, the door does not move. Push in 3 %.

## 04 · s2 the lamp goes out (8.4 s)

**Still:** the doorway seen closer and much darker: only the cold blue opening and the wet floor
boards in front of it, the rest solid black. + global style token

**Motion:** the frame darkens by a stop as the lamp behind camera dies; the night outside gets
slightly brighter as the eye adjusts. No other movement.

## 05 · s3 wet feet cross the floor (13.3 s)

**Still:** extreme low angle on a mud floor: a trail of wet bare footprints walking straight at
the viewer, water glistening, cold light behind them, everything beyond black. + global style token

**Motion:** the last few prints darken and fill with water one after another, closer and closer
to camera; at the end the frame drops as if the camera itself has dived under the bed. Push in 4 %.

## 06 · s4 the cold hand (10.3 s)

**Still:** from under the khat bed: slats and rope webbing overhead, a rolled quilt, a chamber
pot, cobwebs, black depth — and at the left edge a pale grey waterlogged hand with long wet
fingers gripping the bedpost. + global style token

**Motion:** the fingers tighten, once; water runs down the wrist. Creep forward 3 %.

## 07 · s5 two feet at the bed (12.1 s)

**Still:** floor level under the bed: two bare feet standing at the edge of frame, close,
blackened and swollen and waterlogged, a dark puddle spreading, toes bent backwards. Above the
ankles, cut off and dark. + global style token

**Motion:** a small shift of weight from one foot to the other; the puddle spreads; the toes
settle. Camera absolutely still.

## 08 · s6 it kneels, in his voice (11.0 s)

**Still:** from under the bed, a hunched figure on all fours at the bedside, backlit by cold
moonlight, long wet black hair hanging straight down over the face, pale waterlogged limbs, black
wet feet, wet prints behind it. No face, no wounds. + global style token

**Motion:** it lowers its head slowly towards the floor, hair swinging forward, and stops;
water drips from the hair. Push in 3 %.

## 09 · s7 a hand over his mouth (10.1 s)

**Still:** very tight: the dark under the bed, a pale hand clamped over a mouth — the viewer's
own point of view, only the hand and the shadow of a face in the corner of frame, no features.
+ global style token

**Motion:** the hand presses harder; three small twitches as three names are said; the frame
darkens at the edges. Push in 2 %.

## 10 · s8 the azan and the prints (12.2 s)

**Still:** the room at first light, the khat bed empty, the lamp cold, and on the dusty floor two
handprints and drag marks. + global style token

**Motion:** a shriek's worth of dark motion at the edge of frame at the start; then the room
simply sits in the dawn, dust turning in the light. Slow pull back 3 %.

## 11 · s9 the first bus out (9.5 s)

**Still:** inside a battered country bus at dawn: Rafi by the window in three-quarter from
behind, forehead near the glass, exhausted; misty rice fields and palms sliding past outside,
worn green vinyl. + global style token

**Motion:** the fields slide; the bus jolts twice; his reflection does not move when he does.
Camera holds.

## 12 · s10a the phone on the floor (6.5 s)

**Still:** a small city flat at night: narrow bed, ceiling fan, barred window with tower blocks
and sodium light, a phone face up on the floor glowing cold blue, a hard blue rim on the bed leg,
the rest amber-dark. + global style token

**Motion:** the fan turns slowly; the phone screen pulses brighter and dimmer as it rings — but
no sound yet. Push in 4 % toward the phone.

## 13 · s10b the screen: «মা» (5.8 s)

**Still:** a close-up of a phone screen: near-black, a silhouette avatar in a thin circle, the
name **মা** in white, **মোবাইল** under it, red and green call buttons at the bottom. Comic
lettering, no glow gradients. + global style token

**Motion:** the call buttons breathe slowly; at 3.5 s the screen cuts to **কল শেষ · মিসড কল · মা**
for 1.1 s, then rings again. Nothing else moves.

## 14 · s10c the screen: «রাফি» (3.3 s)

**Still:** the same screen, ringing again: **মা** — then, at 2.5 s, the name becomes **রাফি** and
the line under it becomes **আপনি**. + global style token

**Motion:** the change is a hard cut in the UI, not a cross-fade. Then the ring cuts mid-trill at
the end of the clip.

## 15 · out the steel door, three knocks (2.3 s)

**Still:** a city flat's steel front door at night from inside: chain lock, peephole, worn mat,
torn posters, a thin hard line of corridor light under the door — and in that line the shadow of
two feet. + global style token

**Motion:** three knocks; the shadow does not shift between them. Push in 2 %, then cut to black.

## Voice

One narrator for all ten lines, the same voice as part 1 — quiet, close, telling it after the
fact. Lines 4 and 7 include whispers that are *reported*, never acted: the narrator keeps his
composure while describing the worst thing in the room. Do not cut a line at a shot change; the
cuts in this episode land inside the line on purpose.
