## The two voices

Habib is tired, dry, unimpressed by his own death; he never raises his voice, not even at
«তিনশো বছর». The clerk speaks like a form being read out — flat, clipped, bored — and only in the
last scene does he sound like a person, when he says «আজ তো সময় হবে না। **কাল আসুন।**» The joke
is in that warmth, so it must not be sarcastic.

## The rig (why these shots move)

In the render each character is a vector figure (`puppet.py`), not a still: shoulders, elbows, hips
and head are pinned, and every line has its own pose keyframes in shot-relative seconds. Breathing
(0.31 Hz) and a head sway (0.17 Hz) run continuously underneath, so nobody is ever frozen; the
walking cycle in the corridor and the queue is a sine on the hip swing with the arms counter-swinging.

Almost still, all the way through — the comedy is bureaucratic:

- office-earth: fan turning, a paper lifting on the desk, nothing else;
- queue: the queue shuffles half a step, the signboard creaks;
- counter: the clerk's shoulders move once per line, the bell does not ring by itself;
- file-wall: a bundle settles, dust drifts;
- corridor: the bulb swings two inches;
- load-shedding: the frame drops half a stop in one frame — cut, not fade;
- bribe: a coin rolls a centimetre and stops;
- cards: 5 % push, no rotation;
- end-counter: the man does not move at all.
