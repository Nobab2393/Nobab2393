Vertical 1080×1920, Hailuo 2.3 image-to-video. Two styles, on purpose:

- **Scenes** — flat vector noir, thick dark outlines, one flat fill per shape, no gradients.
- **Suspect cards** — the pastel flat-vector bust portraits (your brief), each on its own pastel
  ground with the name, the role, and the one line they said.

**Global style token (scenes):**

> flat vector noir illustration, thick dark outlines, solid flat colours only, no gradients,
> simple geometric shapes, minimal shading; monsoon palette of ink navy, ash grey, bone white and
> one amber lamp; rain as flat white dashes. No text, no watermark.

**Global style token (cards):**

> flat vector cartoon character, thick dark outlines, solid flat colours only, no gradients,
> simple geometric shapes, minimal shading, front-facing bust portrait, head and shoulders
> centred, plain pastel background.

**Negative prompt (both):**

> photorealistic, 3d render, cgi, gradient shading, airbrush, lens flare, halftone dots, blurry,
> soft focus, text, subtitles, watermark, logo, extra limbs, extra fingers, deformed hands,
> morphing faces, flicker, jump cut, camera shake, blood, gore, wounds, neon colours, daylight.
