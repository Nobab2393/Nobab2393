# Director proposal — অন্ধকার পাঠ · স্টিকম্যান (30 s, A1 Dark, 9:16)

**Request:** turn the lesson from episode 02 (“যে দান ফেরত চায়, সেটা দান নয় — ঋণ”) into a
stickman short that could run on Shorts/TikTok, in Bangla, vertical.

**Style:** A1 · Classic Black & White — **Dark variant**: pure black canvas, pure white line
art, one hollow circular head, two arms, two legs, no face, no clothing. Vertical 9:16, composed
natively: figure in the lower two thirds, the rope and its transformation in the upper third,
caption band kept clear.

**Duration:** 3 × 10 s clips (A1's native unit). Total 30 s.

**Voice:** Bangla narration, one fixed voice, native 1×, three lines total — roughly one line per
clip, phrased so the final beat lands on the last four words.

## One graphic metaphor

**A rope.** It is the only object in the film, and it changes state exactly three times:

| state | clip | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| a slack white rope end, handed over | 1 | the gift |
| a small white knot left in his own palm | 1 → 3 | the receipt — the claim he keeps |
| the same rope, thickened into a grey chain, coiled around **his** wrist | 2 | the debt that grew on his side |
| the knot opens, the chain becomes one thin blue line and leaves frame | 3 | giving without an account |

Accent discipline: **electric blue `#2F80FF` is used only for the claim** — the faint glow on the
rope when he keeps the knot, the coil, the final line. Nothing else in the film is blue. Red is
not used at all.

## Beat chain (no beat without a consequence)

| t | picture | carried forward |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | Black frame. A white rope lies across the floor. He walks in, picks up one end, and holds it out. | the offered end |
| 3–7 | A second figure enters shivering (white zigzag tremor), takes the rope. The first figure's palm closes around a small knot. | the knot, and the rope now in someone else's hand |

| 10–13 | Same black frame. The rope is back — thicker, grey, coiled around his own wrist. The far end is gone. | the chain, on the wrong wrist |
| 13–17 | He pulls the chain toward the empty side. Nothing comes. He pulls again; the chain tightens and drags him one step. | his body is now attached to it |
| 17–20 | He stops. The knot in his palm is now the chain's lock. He looks at it. | the knot as the cause, not the relic |
| 20–23 | He opens the palm. The knot drops. | the lock, undone |
| 23–27 | The chain unfurls into one thin blue line and travels off frame, left to right. | release has a direction |
| 27–30 | He walks right, unhurried. Final hold: an empty black frame with the blue line already gone. | nothing owed, nothing held |

## Constraint audit (what will go wrong if unstated)

- No gradients, glow, blur, grain: A1 Dark dies the moment the canvas is not pure black.
- No duplicate or disconnected limbs; each limb exists only in its current pose.
- Exactly two figures; the second has no face and no clothing either — only the tremor reads him.
- The only readable text in the whole film is the ending card **“সেটা দান নয়।”** — every other
  word lives in narration and captions.
- No 3D, no perspective, no motion trails on limbs. The rope may trail; the limbs may not.
- Screen direction is fixed: the second figure and the release move **left → right**.
