# A1 · Dark · Clip 1 — the gift and the receipt (0–10 s)

```text
Create exactly one 10-second vertical 9:16 kinetic line-animation clip with native Bangla
narration and synchronized action sound effects.

STYLE:
Pure black canvas in every frame, pure white line art. The only accent is electric blue #2F80FF,
used once: a faint glow that enters the rope in the last beat, meaning a claim has been kept.
No other colour appears anywhere.
Use minimalist two-dimensional white line animation. Show the same consistent stick figure A:
hollow circular head, no face, no eyes, no mouth, no hair, no clothing, no filled body, stable
proportions about four and a half heads tall, connected line limbs, uniform medium line weight.
Exactly two arms and two legs are visible per figure; each limb appears only in its current pose:
raising an arm replaces that arm's previous line, never leaving a resting arm beneath an active
arm. A second figure B uses the identical drawing, distinguished only by a constant small tremor.
Compose for portrait: the rope and its transformation occupy the upper third, the figures stand in
the lower two thirds, and the bottom 220 pixels stay empty black for captions.

TIMED SEQUENCE:
[0–3s] Wide vertical view of empty black space. One white rope lies slack across the floor at
chest height of the frame. Figure A walks in from the left with a clear step cycle, stops, bends,
picks up one end with two fingers, and straightens. The other end stays on the floor.
[3–7s] Cut closer on contact. Figure A holds the rope end out toward the right. Figure B steps in
from the right, visibly trembling in a small constant zigzag along the shoulders and knees, and
takes the rope end with both hands. In the same beat, A's other hand closes into a fist around a
small knot that appears in that palm. Hold both contacts for a readable moment.
[7–10s] Cut wide. B walks out of frame to the right; the rope pays out and leaves A's outstretched
hand. A stands alone, holding the knot in a closed fist. The knot and the last metre of rope glow
faint electric blue and the glow settles. End on A small in the frame, fist up, rope gone.

VOICEOVER:
Use one warm, low Bangla narrator, same voice in all three clips. Read exactly:
"সে কাঁপছিল। আমি দিলাম। কিন্তু একটা হিসাব মনে রেখে দিলাম।"

AUDIO:
A soft rope drag on a hard floor, one cloth rustle as the rope changes hands, a single small dry
click when the fist closes, then quiet. Narration dominant; no music.

CONSTRAINTS:
Flat 2D side and three-quarter views only. No perspective, extrusion, gradients, glow on limbs,
grain, shadows, textures, 3D, duplicate or disconnected limbs, motion trails on limbs, faces,
clothing, logos, captions, subtitles or watermarks. The only glowing element is the knot and its
short rope end. No readable text in this clip. Keep the canvas pure black in every frame. Do not
alter or repeat the voiceover.
```

## Why these choices

- The knot is introduced **in the same beat** as the handover, so the audience reads “gift + receipt”
  as one act rather than two unrelated props.
- The blue accent is introduced exactly once, at 7 s, and only on the thing he kept — that is what
  makes clips 2 and 3 legible without a word of explanation.
- The portrait composition reserves the bottom band before anything else is designed, so captions
  never have to fight the rope.
- `B` is the same drawing plus a tremor: an A1 cast is one drawing family, not one face for all.
