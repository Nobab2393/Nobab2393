# A1 · Dark · Clip 2 — the chain that grew from it (10–20 s)

```text
Create exactly one 10-second vertical 9:16 kinetic line-animation clip with native Bangla
narration and synchronized action sound effects.

STYLE:
Pure black canvas in every frame, pure white line art. Figure A keeps its exact drawing: hollow
circular head, no face, no clothing, no filled body, stable proportions, connected line limbs,
uniform medium line weight, exactly two arms and two legs, each limb only in its current pose.
The chain is white outline with a flat cool-grey fill, and one short electric-blue #2F80FF segment
marks the link nearest his wrist — the claim, nothing else. No other colour.
Compose for portrait: chain and wrist action in the upper two thirds, A's feet in the lower third,
the bottom 220 pixels empty black for captions.

CONTINUITY:
Begin on the same end state as Clip 1: A alone and small, fist closed around the glowing knot,
the rope already gone from frame.

TIMED SEQUENCE:
[0–3s] Hold the Clip 1 end frame for a beat, then the rope returns from frame right — but thicker.
It drags across the floor as a heavy chain, its far end frayed and ending in nothing. The chain
coils twice around A's wrist and stops.
[3–7s] Cut close on the wrist. A pulls the chain toward the empty right side with both hands; the
links lift and snap back, and the blue link flares once. He pulls again, harder, feet braced. The
chain tightens and drags him one full step to the right, skidding.
[7–10s] Cut wide. A stops, breathing, still attached. He turns his closed fist palm-up and opens
it: the knot is not a relic any more, it is the lock the chain runs through, and the blue light
sits inside it. End on the open palm with the knot glowing, chain slack around his wrist.

VOICEOVER:
The same Bangla narrator. Read exactly:
"বছর গেল। দানটা তার কাছে গল্প হলো। আমার কাছে হলো দেনা।"

AUDIO:
A heavy chain settling, a dry metal scrape as it drags, one strained creak on each pull, a short
skid and a footstep as he is dragged, then the quiet tick of the chain going slack. Narration
dominant; no music.

CONSTRAINTS:
Flat 2D views only. No perspective, extrusion, gradients, glow on limbs, grain, shadows, 3D,
duplicate or disconnected limbs, motion trails on limbs, faces, clothing, logos, captions,
subtitles or watermarks. The chain may trail; limbs may not. No readable text. Keep the canvas
pure black. Do not alter or repeat the voiceover.
```

## Why these choices

- The transformation is **a change of the same object**, not a new prop: the rope returns thicker.
  A1's whole grammar is “one object, changing state”, and it removes any need for exposition.
- He pulls **toward empty space** — the consequence of that pull is his own body moving. That is
  the visual sentence “the debt is on my side now”.
- The knot payoff is moved here, not to the end: the audience must understand *why* the chain is
  attached before the release can feel earned.
