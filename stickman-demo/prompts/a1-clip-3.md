# A1 · Dark · Clip 3 — the knot opens (20–30 s)

```text
Create exactly one 10-second vertical 9:16 kinetic line-animation clip with native Bangla
narration, synchronized action sound effects, and one short on-screen line of Bangla text.

STYLE:
Pure black canvas in every frame, pure white line art. Figure A keeps its exact drawing: hollow
circular head, no face, no clothing, no filled body, stable proportions, connected line limbs,
uniform medium line weight, exactly two arms and two legs, each limb only in its current pose.
Electric blue #2F80FF marks only the claim: the knot, then the single line it becomes. Nothing
else is blue. The closing text is the only readable text in the film.
Compose for portrait: the palm and chain in the upper two thirds, the walk in the lower third, the
bottom 220 pixels empty black except for the closing line, which is centred and short.

CONTINUITY:
Begin on the Clip 2 end state: A stands still, palm open, the glowing knot resting in it, the
chain slack around his wrist.

TIMED SEQUENCE:
[0–3s] Close on the open palm. A's other hand comes up, takes the knot between two fingers, and
pulls one loop loose. The knot comes apart in three readable tugs.
[3–7s] Cut wide as the moment it fails. The blue light leaves the knot and runs down the chain
link by link; the chain unlinks, thins, and becomes a single thin blue line that whips free and
travels left to right, off frame. A's wrist is bare. The last link drops and disappears.
[7–10s] A turns and walks right at an unhurried pace, step cycle clear, no looking back. The empty
black frame holds for a moment after he exits. Then the short closing line appears alone, centred
in the caption band: "সেটা দান নয়।" Hold on the black frame and the text.

VOICEOVER:
The same Bangla narrator. Read exactly:
"যে দান ফেরত চাই, সেটা দান নয়। গিঁটটা খুলে দিলাম — দড়িটাই তখন হালকা হলো।"

AUDIO:
A fine rope rasp on each tug, one clean release as the line whips free, a light metallic drop, then
three unhurried footsteps that fade. Narration dominant; no music.

CONSTRAINTS:
Flat 2D views only. No perspective, extrusion, gradients, glow on limbs, grain, shadows, 3D,
duplicate or disconnected limbs, motion trails on limbs, faces, clothing, logos, captions,
subtitles or watermarks. The only readable text is "সেটা দান নয়।" — nothing else. Keep the canvas
pure black in every frame. Do not alter or repeat the voiceover.
```

## Why these choices

- Three tugs give the release a **duration and a rhythm**; a single gesture would read as a cut.
- The blue light *travelling* down the chain carries the meaning into the next state — the claim
  does not vanish, it leaves.
- He never looks back: the correction is not revenge, which is what keeps the ending from reading
  as a win over somebody.
- The closing line is one short Bangla sentence in the caption band, reserved from the start, so it
  is never over his face or the rope.
