# MiniMax / Hailuo shot pack — স্পেস স্টোরি · অতিথি ২

Fourteen clips to rebuild this film in Hailuo 2.3 (image-to-video, vertical). One still + one
motion prompt per clip. Cut on the in/out times, lay `build/mix.wav` (or the ten
`audio/sceneNN.mp3` at the `plan.py` start times) underneath, and the narration sits exactly
where it sits in the local render.

**Global style token** (end of every still prompt):

> 1970s science-fiction comic book illustration, thick confident black ink outlines of even
> weight, flat poster colours only — mustard yellow, burnt orange, cream, teal, steel grey —
> solid black shadows, one flat highlight band per surface, a hint of off-register print at the
> edges, faint paper tooth. No gradients, no airbrush, no photorealism, no halftone dots.

**Global negative prompt:**

> photorealistic, 3d render, cgi, glossy skin, blurry, soft focus, gradient shading, airbrush,
> halftone dots, lens flare, modern digital look, text, subtitles, watermark, logo, extra limbs,
> extra fingers, deformed hands, morphing faces, flicker, jump cut, camera shake, crowd, second
> alien in frame unless listed, earth in the sky, two moons.

**Camera rules:** 1080×1920, one slow move per clip — push-in ≤ 4 % or 2° drift. No handheld,
no whip pans, no crash zooms. People act with neck, shoulders and one hand; nobody walks except
where the table says so.

**Continuity:** the guest is the same creature as in part 12 — grey-teal skin, three long
fingers, two big black almond eyes, fine spines down the spine. The small one is the same
design at about half height. The ship has one corridor, one bridge, one engine room, one cabin,
one observation window; the same red emergency lamp, the same cream paint gone yellow, the same
open floor grille. Mars is a small red dot that never moves. No one wears a helmet inside.

## 01 · window, stars (5.8 s)

**Still:** inside a small deep-space ship, a big oval observation window with a thick riveted
steel frame, a low padded bench beneath it, a handrail, a wall lamp switched off; through the
glass a deep black sky, a scatter of stars, a faint violet nebula at the edge and a small red
planet far behind. No people. + global style token

**Motion:** stars hold; the faint nebula breathes; a soft blue glow crawls along the handrail.
Camera pushes in 3 %. Empty frame the whole way.

## 02 · corridor, grille open (5.3 s)

**Still:** a narrow ribbed ship corridor running away from the viewer, pipes and cable bundles,
a steel floor grille lifted out of its bed and lying ajar, hazard stripes, one red emergency
lamp glowing, a hatch with a wheel handle at the far end. No people. + global style token

**Motion:** the red lamp pulses twice, a thin vapour seeps out of the open floor, dust in the
lamp's cone. Camera drifts forward 2 %.

## 03 · s1 bridge, Mars shrinking (9.8 s)

**Motion:** the scope sweeps once; the commander turns her head to the window and back; the
