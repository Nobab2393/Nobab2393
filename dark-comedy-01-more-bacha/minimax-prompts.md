## Motion notes

Everything moves a little and nothing moves fast — this is a deadpan film:

- graveyard / janaza / jail: a 2–3 % push, one small breath in the crowd;
- coffin-pov: nothing but a grain of light moving in the slit;
- pill: a 3 % push on the tablet;
- coffin-burst: the lid swings 15°, his hair settles — no shake, no zoom-punch;
- car-lane: the headlights do not blink; the two figures stay frozen mid-run in the beams;
- cards: a 5 % push, no rotation.

## Dialogue

One voice, deadpan, unhurried; the jokes are carried by the pauses, not by emphasis. Say the last
line («মরে গেলেও দেনা মরে না») a touch slower than everything before it.
