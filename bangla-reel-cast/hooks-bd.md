# ৪৭টা হুক · এই পাঁচ মুখ দিয়ে
## The 47 hooks, adapted to Bangla and matched to the cast

Every hook below is the same hook you sent, rewritten to fit **2 seconds of Bangla on-screen
type at 1080×1920** — short enough to read, long enough to open a gap. `[ ]` is the one thing
you fill in. The right-hand column is who says it: the voice on the track and the face on
screen.

The rule for all of them: **the hook is a line of dialogue, not a title.** The character is
already looking at the viewer and mid-sentence; the words land on top.

## How to spend the 2 seconds

```
0.0 – 0.4 s   face already on screen, mouth open, mid-word      (no logo, no title)
0.0 – 2.0 s   the hook, on screen, max 2 lines of Bangla
2.0 – 3.5 s   the proof: one number, one object, one photo
3.5 – 5.0 s   the turn: "কিন্তু আসল কারণটা এই"
```

The face must move on frame one. A still image with a caption is the thing these hooks are
fighting against — that is why the cast is designed with mouths, hands and props that can be
animated rather than poster portraits.
