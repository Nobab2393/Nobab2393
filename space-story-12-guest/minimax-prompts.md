# MiniMax / Hailuo shot pack — স্পেস স্টোরি · পর্ব ১২ «অতিথি»

Fourteen clips that rebuild this film in Hailuo 2.3 (image-to-video, vertical). Every clip is
one still + one motion prompt. Cut the clips on the in/out times below, lay the local
`build/mix.wav` under them (or the ten `audio/sceneNN.mp3` files at the timings in
`plan.py`), and the lip-free narration lines up exactly as in the local render.

**Global style token** (paste at the end of every still prompt):

> 1970s science-fiction comic book illustration, thick confident black ink outlines of even
> weight, flat poster colours only — mustard yellow, burnt orange, cream, teal, steel grey —
> solid black shadows, one flat highlight band per surface, a hint of off-register print at
> the edges, faint paper tooth. No gradients, no airbrush, no photorealism, no halftone dots.

**Global negative prompt:**

> photorealistic, 3d render, cgi, glossy plastic skin, blurry, soft focus, gradient shading,
> airbrush, halftone dots, lens flare, modern digital look, text, subtitles, watermark, logo,
> extra limbs, extra fingers, deformed hands, morphing faces, flicker, jump cut, camera shake,
> crowds, second alien, two moons, earth in the sky.

**Camera rules** (keep every clip in one grammar): 1080×1920, 24 fps look, one slow move per
clip — a push-in no faster than 4 % or a 2° drift — no handheld, no whip pans, no zoom-punches.
Figures stand still and act with the neck, shoulders and one hand; nothing walks except where
the table says so.

**Continuity:** the guest never touches the bell/console on camera; the wall clock reads 2:10 in
every interior clip and only the dawn clip shows the sun; the guest's three fingers and two big
black almond eyes are identical in every appearance; the colony's red dust is the only warm
saturated colour; the hero helmets stay under the arm, never worn.

## 01 · prologue — dome asleep (5.2 s)

**Still:** interior of a small pressurised Mars habitat at night, curved tin ribs overhead, five
narrow bunks along the walls, steel lockers, a round porthole showing black space, a bolted
round table with a steel teapot, a red warning lamp over the airlock hatch at the back, a single
hanging bulb as the key, deep shadow in the corners. No people. + global style token

**Motion:** the hanging bulb swings two degrees, dust drifts in its cone, the red lamp pulses
once. Camera pushes in 3 %. Nothing else moves.

## 02 · prologue — inner airlock open (4.8 s)

**Still:** inside a Mars airlock, the heavy round pressure door standing wide open onto blackness,
brass wheel handle, frost on the seal, hazard stripes on the floor, red warning lamp above,
coiled hoses, red dust blown across the floor in a fan. No people. + global style token

**Motion:** the open door breathes a thin vapour, the red lamp flickers twice, dust lifts off the
floor and settles. Camera drifts left 2°. No one enters frame.

## 03 · s1 dome, five awake (11.4 s)

**Still:** the same dome, now with five people standing in the warm light facing the camera —
a 48-year-old Bengali woman commander in a burnt-orange pressure suit with a helmet under her
arm, a 35-year-old engineer in mustard overalls with goggles on his forehead and a wrench, a
31-year-old woman doctor in a cream smock and stethoscope with a boxy medical case, a 26-year-old
pilot in an orange flight jacket and leather flying cap, a 19-year-old comms tech in a teal
jumpsuit with a headset and an antenna pack holding a notebook. All five watchful, nobody
speaking. + global style token

**Motion:** each of them breathes; the commander turns her head slowly to the hatch and back; the
teen looks down at his notebook. Camera pushes in 4 %. Hold the group exactly as composed — no
one walks.

## 04 · s2 three-toed prints (9.4 s)

**Still:** extreme low view of a metal floor plate dusted with fine red-orange dust, a line of
small three-toed bare footprints crossing the frame, the nearest print large and sharp in the
foreground, a human boot print at the edge for scale, rivets, one hard lamp from the upper left.
+ global style token

**Motion:** dust slides a centimetre across the plate; the lamp above sways and the shadows of
the prints swing slowly. Camera creeps forward 5 % along the line of prints.

## 05 · s3 five torches, moving (9.3 s)

**Still:** the dome at night seen a little wider, three of the five crossing the room with
torches, a wrench and a length of iron bar, the others behind them at the bunks, hard torch light
throwing long shadows across the curved wall. + global style token

**Motion:** the three walk forward on a slow, heavy step cycle (one step per 0.46 s), torch beams
sweep the wall, the commander looks left then right. Camera tracks back 2 %.

## 06 · s4 greenhouse, drip (7.8 s)

**Still:** inside the low plastic greenhouse tunnel, two long beds of tomato plants, grow lamps on
wires, a big cylindrical water tank with a gauge, a dripping pipe and a puddle, red dust tracked
in a trail; the doctor in the cream smock at the left, the commander beside her, and far right a
thin grey-teal three-fingered creature half-hidden by the tank, dimly lit. + global style token

**Motion:** a drop falls and rings in the puddle; the lamp above flickers once; the creature turns
its big head toward the women and freezes. Camera pushes in 3 %.

## 07 · s5 shock, the guest runs (7.8 s)

**Still:** inside the dusty rover bay, the engineer in mustard overalls recoiling from a live
cable with a bright spark at his hand, a huge six-wheeled rover behind him, the pilot with a torch
at the right, and at the far right the creature sprinting away in a blur of thin limbs, tool
cabinets and gas cylinders around them. + global style token

**Motion:** the spark crackles and dies, the engineer staggers back one step and steadies on a
cabinet, the creature crosses the back of the bay left to right in three quick strides, its head
turning back once. Camera pushes in 4 %.

## 08 · s6 bay door down (11.5 s)

**Still:** the rover bay with its huge segmented outer door halfway down on hazard-striped rails,
the commander standing square in the foreground with her helmet under her arm, the doctor beside
her with the medical case, a pressure gauge panel lit, and behind the tool cart at the right a
barely visible three-fingered silhouette crouched low. + global style token

**Motion:** the door keeps descending on its screw, dust puffs from the rails, the gauge needle
falls, the crouched silhouette shivers once and is still. Camera holds absolutely still, then
pushes in 2 % at the very end.

## 09 · s7 Shishir's notebook (8.5 s)

**Still:** medium shot of the 19-year-old comms tech in the teal jumpsuit standing in the bay

**Motion:** he turns one page, traces a line of numbers with the pencil, swallows, and looks up at

## 10 · s8 the guest in the light (8.3 s)

**Still:** the bay again; centre frame the grey-teal three-fingered creature standing fully in the

**Motion:** the creature's chest rises and falls fast, its head tilts 4°, one three-fingered hand
opens and closes; the light behind it steadies. Camera drifts right 2°.

## 11 · s9 the door goes up (10.2 s)

**Still:** the rover bay, the outer door rising on its rails and red dawn-grey light coming in

**Motion:** the door climbs, dust and vapour blow inward, the creature takes four slow steps

## 12 · s10 dawn at the glass (13.1 s)

**Still:** from inside the colony, looking out through a wide curved glass panel at the red Mars
plain at sunrise, the antenna mast and a small flag in the middle distance, rover tracks in the
dust, a pale sun on the ridge; the commander and the comms tech standing inside at the bottom of
frame, and far out on the dust a small grey-teal three-fingered figure facing the glass.
+ global style token

**Motion:** the sun lifts two degrees, wind moves the dust in long sheets, the two inside breathe
and do not speak; at 9.2 s the distant figure raises one three-fingered hand toward the glass and
holds it there. Camera pushes in 3 % over the whole clip.

## 13 · out — empty room, red plain (2.5 s)

**Still:** the same window a minute later, the two gone, one folded chair, the lamp off, the plain
empty except the tracks. + global style token

**Motion:** only the dust moves; the frame slowly brightens by half a stop.
