# MiniMax / Hailuo shot pack — ভয়ের গল্প · পর্ব ৩ «তৃতীয় ডাক»

Fifteen generated clips + one screen UI + one end card. Vertical 1080×1920, Hailuo 2.3
image-to-video, one slow move per clip. Cut on the in/out times, lay `build/mix.wav` under them.

**Global style token** (end of every still prompt):

> 1970s Bengali horror comic book illustration, thick confident black ink outlines of even
> weight, flat poster colours only — amber, burnt umber, mustard, deep olive green, bone cream,
> cold grey-blue night and cold blue phone light — solid black shadows, one flat highlight band
> per surface, a hint of off-register print, faint paper tooth. No gradients, no airbrush, no
> photorealism, no halftone dots.

**Global negative prompt:**

> photorealistic, 3d render, cgi, glossy skin, blurry, soft focus, gradient shading, airbrush,
> halftone dots, lens flare, modern digital look, text, subtitles, watermark, extra limbs, extra
> fingers, deformed hands, morphing faces, flicker, jump cut, camera shake, blood, gore, wounds,
> a visible face on the creature, daylight, neon colours.

**Camera rules:** one move per clip, push-in ≤ 4 % — except the last two clips, which must push
continuously and match-cut into each other (same plate, same centre, zoom continuing 1.10 → 1.30).
The three calls are the edit: each one is closer than the one before.

**Continuity:** the same Rafi as parts 1–2 (cream shirt, stubble, bare feet in the village;
shirt and trousers in the city). Mother is only ever a silhouette seen from behind — never a
face. The thing at the child's door is a flat black shape with long wet hair and no features.
The village flashback is warm amber lamp light; the flat is amber with cold blue phone light.
The mirror is the same mirror in both mirror shots: thin wooden frame on an orange plaster wall.

## 01 · p0 the phone on the floor (2.8 s)

**Still:** a small Dhaka flat at night: narrow bed, ceiling fan, barred window with a cold orange
street glow, a smartphone lying face up on the floor, its screen lit pale blue, everything else
black. + global style token

**Motion:** the screen dims and brightens with the ring; the fan turns slowly. Camera still.

## 02 · p1 push toward the phone (2.3 s)

**Still:** same room, closer on the phone on the boards, the pale screen lighting a small ring of
dust and a dead moth. + global style token

**Motion:** push in 4 %, unhurried; the screen light pulses once.

## 03 · s1 the hook (9.2 s)

**Still:** close on Rafi, 37, cream shirt damp at the collar, three days of stubble, a phone
pressed hard against his ear, eyes wide, tears standing in them, cold blue light under his jaw,
amber on the other side, the room black behind him. + global style token

**Motion:** he blinks once, swallows, the phone shifts a millimetre against his ear; his shadow
breathes on the wall. Push in 2 %.

## 04 · s2 the call screen (7.8 s)

**Still (code, not generated):** near-black screen, silhouette avatar in a thin circle, **মা**,
**সংযুক্ত**, a running timer, one red end-call button, and a band of line noise across the middle.
Comic lettering, no glow gradients.

**Motion:** the timer counts; the noise band jitters — always different, never resolving into a
voice.

## 05 · s3a the fever (8.8 s)

**Still:** village room at night, a small boy of about seven under a thin quilt with a wet cloth
on his forehead, a hurricane lamp burning low beside him, and a woman in a cotton sari kneeling
at the bed — only her back and her hair in a bun, no face. + global style token

**Motion:** the lamp flame dips; the woman's shoulders rise and fall once; the boy's chest moves.
Push in 2 %.

## 06 · s3b the figure in the doorway (9.4 s)

**Still:** from the child's bed: the low doorway filled with cold blue night, and standing in it
a tall thin black silhouette with long straight wet hair over its face, arms hanging too long,
pale wet feet on the threshold. No features, no eyes. + global style token

**Motion:** the figure does not move at all; the cold light behind it swells slightly, as if
someone further out had stepped closer. Camera holds, then creeps in 2 %.

## 07 · s3c his mother answering (11.4 s)

**Still:** the same woman from behind, at the doorway, one hand flat on the frame, shawl over one
shoulder, facing out into the dark where only cold blue light and faint rain are visible. Warm
lamp light behind her throws her shadow forward across the floor. + global style token

**Motion:** her hand tightens on the frame; the shawl moves in the draught; the shadow on the
floor lengthens. Push in 2 %.

## 08 · s4 thirty years, and 37 (13.0 s)

**Still:** tighter on Rafi with the phone at his ear, now seen slightly from below: the jaw
working, the eyes going from wet to blank as he does the arithmetic. + global style token

**Motion:** he looks down and away, then back at nothing; one slow blink. Push in 3 %.

## 09 · s5 three knocks on the flat door (13.3 s)

**Still:** the steel front door of the flat from inside at night: chain lock, peephole, worn mat,
torn posters, and a thin hard line of corridor light under it — with the shadow of two feet
standing in that line. + global style token

**Motion:** three knocks land; the shadow does not shift between them; dust falls from the frame
on the third. Push in 2 %.

## 10 · s6 the second call, from behind (10.6 s)

**Still:** a cheap wall mirror, thin wooden frame, on an orange plaster wall; in the glass, the
reflection of Rafi sitting on the edge of the bed, head lowered, phone loose in his hand; behind
him the room recedes into darkness. A shirt hangs on a nail beside the mirror. + global style token

**Motion:** the hanging shirt moves; the reflection's shoulders lift with a breath; nothing else.
Push in 2 %.

## 11 · s7a the third call, at the ear (9.0 s)

**Still:** the same mirror, tighter on the glass: the reflection of Rafi's head and shoulder, and
in the dark behind his reflection, the suggestion of the room going on and on. + global style token

**Motion:** the reflection's head tilts very slightly, as if listening. Push in 3 %.

## 12 · s7b the reflection, turned (7.5 s)

**Still:** the same mirror and the same frame — but the reflection is now facing straight out of
the glass: head up, eyes wide and calm, long wet black hair over a pale damp forehead, mouth just
opening, water dripping onto the shirt. + global style token

**Motion:** a single drip falls; the eyes do not blink. Push in 2 %. **Hard cut from clip 11 — no
dissolve.**

## 13 · s8 «হ্যাঁ?» (1.9 s)

**Still:** closer still on that reflection, the wet face filling the glass. + global style token

**Motion:** the lips part on the word, then close. Nothing else in the frame moves.

## 14 · s9 the new voice (11.1 s)

**Still:** extreme close-up of Rafi's face in the dark, wet strands of hair on his forehead, skin
pale and damp, eyes wide and perfectly still, the smallest beginning of a smile, cold blue rim on
one side, amber on the other, black behind. + global style token

**Motion:** he does not blink, does not breathe visibly; only the highlights on the wet skin
shift as the light does. Push in 2 % — and end at zoom 1.10 for the match cut.

## 15 · s10 the push that does not stop (9.3 s)

**Still:** the identical plate and framing as clip 14. + global style token

**Motion:** the camera keeps moving, 1.10 → 1.30, until the face fills the frame and there is
nothing else to look at. **Match cut, not a new shot.**

## 16 · out black + a door opening (2.1 s)

Black. One long creak of a door opening somewhere in the flat, already fading. No image.

## 17 · card «আপনার নাম কী?» (3.4 s)

**Still:** flat cream card, hairline ink frame, heavy Bengali line **আপনার নাম কী?** over a
burnt-orange rule, then **ভয়ের গল্প · পর্ব ৩ · তৃতীয় ডাক**, then two small lines:
**তিনবার ডাকলে…** / **তৃতীয়বার সাড়া দিলে?**

**Motion:** none. Hold; on the whisper «আমি শুনেছি» the card should stay absolutely still — the
silence is the last effect.

## Voice

One narrator, all ten lines, the same voice as parts 1 and 2 — but the last two lines are the
same recording **pitched down about 7 % with the length unchanged**, so the calm arrives without
a new actor. Do not re-record them in another voice; the twist is that the same throat keeps
talking.
